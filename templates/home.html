How a complaint moves

Every complaint goes through the same checks, so nothing is marked done until it really is.

  1. 1

    Student reports

    Describe the problem, add the location and choose how urgent it is.

  2. 2

    Maintenance fixes

    The admin is kept informed while maintenance repairs the issue.

  3. 3

    Tutor verifies

    The tutor checks the work, or sends it back if it is not right.

  4. 4

    HOD approves

    The Head of Department gives the final approval.

  5. 5

    Student is told

    The complaint is marked resolved and the student gets a notification.
